Invoicing, Receipts, and Tax Documentation

Accessing Past Invoices
All past invoices are available at Account Settings > Billing > Invoice
History, downloadable as PDF. Invoices are generated automatically on each
billing date and emailed to the billing contact on file at the same time.

Adding Tax Information (VAT/GST/Tax ID)
You can add your VAT number, GST number, or tax ID under Account Settings >
Billing > Tax Information. This information appears on all future invoices
but cannot be retroactively applied to invoices already issued - if you
need a corrected past invoice with tax information added, contact billing
support directly with the specific invoice number.

Sales Tax and VAT Charges
Whether tax is charged on your subscription depends on your billing
location and applicable tax law. For EU customers, VAT is charged unless a
valid VAT number is provided (reverse charge mechanism applies for B2B EU
transactions outside our home country). For US customers, sales tax is
charged based on state-level nexus rules, which vary by state.

Requesting a Different Billing Contact or Address
The billing contact email and address can be updated at any time under
Account Settings > Billing > Billing Details. Changes apply to all future
invoices; past invoices are not regenerated with the new details.

Custom Invoicing (Purchase Orders, Net-30 Terms)
Enterprise customers can request custom invoicing arrangements, including
purchase order numbers on invoices or Net-30/Net-60 payment terms instead
of automatic card billing. These arrangements must be set up by your
account manager and are not available through self-serve billing settings.

Refund Documentation
When a refund is issued, a separate credit note is generated and emailed
alongside the original invoice reference. Credit notes are also available
in Invoice History for your accounting records.
