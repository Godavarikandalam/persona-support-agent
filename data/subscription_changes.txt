Upgrading, Downgrading, and Switching Plans

Upgrading Your Plan
Upgrades take effect immediately upon confirmation at Account Settings >
Billing > Change Plan. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder
of your current billing cycle at the new plan's rate, calculated to the day.
All features of the new plan become available immediately - there is no
waiting period.

Downgrading Your Plan
Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle, not
immediately, so you retain access to your current plan's features until
then. If your current usage exceeds the limits of the plan you're
downgrading to (for example, you have more team seats than the lower plan
allows), you'll be prompted to first remove the excess usage before the
downgrade can be scheduled.

Switching Between Monthly and Annual Billing
Switching from monthly to annual billing applies the annual discount (15%)
starting from your next renewal date and requires no immediate action - the
new annual invoice is generated automatically. Switching from annual back to
monthly is only possible at the end of your current annual term; mid-term
switches to monthly billing are not supported.

Seat-Based Pricing and Adding Team Members
Professional and Enterprise plans are billed per active seat. Adding a new
team member immediately adds a prorated charge for that seat for the
remainder of the billing cycle. Removing a team member does not generate an
immediate refund for that seat, but reduces the seat count (and therefore
the charge) starting the following billing cycle.

Trial Period Conversions
Free trials last 14 days and automatically convert to a paid Professional
plan subscription at the end of the trial unless canceled beforehand from
Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Trial. We send reminder emails 3 days
and 1 day before the trial ends.

Multiple Workspaces Under One Billing Account
Enterprise customers can manage multiple workspaces under a single
consolidated invoice. New workspaces can be added at Dashboard > Organization
> Workspaces > Add Workspace, and each workspace's seat usage is billed
separately within the consolidated invoice for easier cost tracking.
