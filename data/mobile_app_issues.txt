Mobile App Troubleshooting

App Crashes on Launch
If the app crashes immediately on opening, first confirm you're running the
latest version from the App Store or Google Play - we release bug fixes
roughly every two weeks. If you're already on the latest version, try
clearing the app cache (Android: Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Storage >
Clear Cache) or reinstalling (iOS does not support cache-clearing without a
full reinstall).

Push Notifications Not Arriving
Push notifications can fail to arrive for several reasons:
1. Notifications are disabled at the OS level (check Settings > Notifications
   on your device).
2. Battery optimization settings on Android are aggressively killing the
   app's background process - disable "battery optimization" specifically
   for our app in Android Settings > Battery.
3. You are logged into a different account on this device than the one
   notifications are configured for.
4. Do Not Disturb or Focus Mode is active and blocking the notification.

Sync Issues Between Mobile and Web
Changes made on the mobile app typically sync to the web dashboard within
10-15 seconds when you have an active internet connection. If changes
aren't appearing on the other platform:
- Pull down to refresh on mobile, or refresh the browser tab on web.
- Confirm both devices are logged into the same account (not a secondary
  team member account).
- Check for a "Sync paused" banner, which appears when the app has been in
  airplane mode or offline for an extended period.

Biometric Login (Face ID / Fingerprint) Not Working
Biometric login must be re-enabled after any password change for security
reasons. Go to App Settings > Security > Enable Biometric Login to turn it
back on. If your device's biometric data was reset at the OS level (e.g.
after adding a new fingerprint), you may need to disable and re-enable this
setting in the app.

Offline Mode Limitations
The mobile app supports limited offline functionality: you can view
previously loaded data, but cannot create new records or submit forms while
offline. Any draft changes made offline are saved locally and will attempt
to sync automatically once connectivity is restored, but drafts older than
24 hours are discarded for data consistency reasons.
