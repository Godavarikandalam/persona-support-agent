Data Export, Privacy, and GDPR Requests

Exporting Your Data
You can export all of your account data at any time from Account Settings >
Privacy > Export My Data. This generates a ZIP file containing your data in
CSV and JSON formats, and is emailed to your account email address within
30 minutes for accounts with less than 10,000 records, or within 24 hours
for larger accounts.

Right to Erasure (GDPR / "Right to be Forgotten")
Under GDPR and similar privacy regulations, you may request full deletion
of your personal data. Submit this request via Account Settings > Privacy >
Delete My Data, or by emailing privacy@example.com. Because this is a
legally binding, irreversible action involving identity verification, these
requests are always handled by a human privacy team member - they are never
processed automatically through chat support.

Data Retention Periods
- Active account data: retained for the lifetime of the account.
- Deleted account data: retained for 30 days in a recoverable state, then
  permanently purged.
- Support ticket history: retained for 3 years for quality and compliance
  purposes, even after account deletion, unless a specific erasure request
  is honored.
- Billing records: retained for 7 years to comply with financial
  recordkeeping regulations, and cannot be deleted via a standard erasure
  request due to legal obligations.

Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
Enterprise customers requiring a signed Data Processing Agreement for
compliance purposes (e.g. GDPR Article 28) can request one through their
account manager. Self-serve and Professional plan customers can find our
standard DPA template at Dashboard > Legal > Data Processing Agreement.

Cookie and Tracking Preferences
You can manage cookie and analytics tracking preferences at any time via
the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer of our website, or within the
app at Account Settings > Privacy > Cookie Preferences. Essential cookies
required for login and security cannot be disabled.

Sub-processor List
We maintain a public, up-to-date list of all third-party sub-processors
(e.g. cloud hosting, email delivery, analytics providers) who may process
customer data on our behalf, available at Dashboard > Legal > Sub-processors.
