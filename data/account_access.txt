Account Access and Login Troubleshooting

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Lockout
If you are locked out due to a lost 2FA device, use one of your 10 backup
codes generated when you first enabled 2FA (Account Settings > Security >
Backup Codes). If you have lost both your device and your backup codes,
account recovery requires identity verification and must be handled by a
human support agent for security reasons - this cannot be resolved
automatically.

"Account Temporarily Locked" Message
After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, accounts are locked for 30
minutes as a brute-force protection measure. You can wait for the lockout
to expire, or use the "Forgot Password" flow to reset your password
immediately, which also clears the lockout.

Email Verification Not Received
Verification emails can take up to 10 minutes to arrive. If it does not
arrive, check your spam/junk folder for emails from noreply@example.com,
and confirm your email provider is not blocking our sending domain. You can
request a new verification email from the login screen by clicking
"Resend verification email" (limited to one resend every 5 minutes).

Single Sign-On (SSO) Errors
Enterprise plan customers using SAML-based SSO who see an "SSO
configuration error" should verify that the Entity ID and ACS URL in their
identity provider match exactly what is shown in Dashboard > Settings > SSO
Configuration. Mismatched URLs are the most common cause of SSO login
failures.

Session Expiration
Sessions expire after 14 days of inactivity, or immediately if you change
your password. If you are unexpectedly logged out across all devices, this
is expected behavior after a password change and is a security feature, not
a bug.
