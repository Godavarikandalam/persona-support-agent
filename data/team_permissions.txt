Team Roles, Permissions, and Access Management

Available Roles
- Owner: full access including billing, account deletion, and the ability
  to transfer ownership. Only one Owner per account.
- Admin: full access to settings and team management, but cannot delete
  the account or change billing details.
- Member: standard access to core features, no access to billing or team
  management settings.
- Viewer: read-only access, cannot create or edit records.

Changing a Team Member's Role
Only Owners and Admins can change roles, via Dashboard > Team > [Member
Name] > Change Role. Role changes take effect immediately and do not
require the affected member to log out and back in.

Removing a Team Member
Removing a team member immediately revokes their access and ends any
active sessions they have. Their historical activity (comments, created
records) remains in the account and is attributed to their name, but they
can no longer log in. Seat-based billing adjusts starting the next cycle as
described in the subscription changes guide.

Transferring Account Ownership
Ownership transfer requires the current Owner to initiate the transfer
(Dashboard > Team > Transfer Ownership) and the new Owner to accept an
emailed confirmation link within 7 days. This is a sensitive, irreversible
action and our support team cannot initiate or approve a transfer on your
behalf without going through this verification flow.

Custom Permission Sets (Enterprise Only)
Enterprise accounts can create custom permission sets beyond the four
standard roles, allowing fine-grained control (e.g. "can edit records but
not delete them," or "can view billing but not modify it"). These are
configured under Dashboard > Team > Custom Roles.

Audit Log
All permission and role changes are recorded in the audit log (Dashboard >
Security > Audit Log), available on Professional and Enterprise plans,
showing who made the change, when, and what the previous value was.
