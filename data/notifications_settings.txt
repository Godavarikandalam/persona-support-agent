Notification Settings and Email Preferences

Types of Notifications
- Transactional emails (password resets, billing receipts, security
  alerts): cannot be disabled, as they're required for account security and
  legal compliance.
- Product notifications (new comments, status changes, mentions): fully
  configurable per notification type.
- Marketing emails (product updates, newsletters, promotions): can be
  unsubscribed from at any time via the link at the bottom of any marketing
  email, or from Account Settings > Notifications > Marketing Preferences.

Configuring Notification Channels
For each product notification type, you can independently choose to
receive it via email, in-app notification, push notification (mobile), or
any combination of the three, under Account Settings > Notifications >
Channels.

Digest Mode
If you find individual notifications overwhelming, enable Digest Mode
(Account Settings > Notifications > Digest Mode) to receive a single
summary email every morning instead of individual real-time emails. Digest
Mode does not apply to urgent/security notifications, which always send
immediately regardless of this setting.

Notification Delays or Missing Notifications
If you're not receiving expected notifications:
1. Confirm the notification type is enabled in Account Settings >
   Notifications > Channels.
2. Check spam/junk folders for emails from notifications@example.com.
3. For push notifications specifically, see the Mobile App Troubleshooting
   guide for OS-level settings that commonly block delivery.
4. Confirm you haven't muted the specific record/thread the notification
   would relate to (muted items show a small bell-with-slash icon).

Team-Wide Notification Defaults (Admin Only)
Admins can set default notification preferences for new team members under
Dashboard > Team > Notification Defaults. This only affects new members
going forward and does not retroactively change existing members' personal
preferences.
