Billing Policy and Payment FAQ

Subscription Plans
We offer three subscription tiers: Starter ($9/month), Professional ($29/month),
and Enterprise (custom pricing). All plans are billed monthly in advance on the
date you originally subscribed. Annual billing is available for a 15% discount
on Professional and Enterprise plans.

Failed Payments
If a payment fails, we will automatically retry the charge three times over
seven days. You will receive an email notification after each failed attempt.
If all three retries fail, your account will be downgraded to the free tier
until a valid payment method is added.

Duplicate Charges
Duplicate charges typically occur when a payment is retried by your bank or
when a subscription change (upgrade/downgrade) overlaps with a billing cycle.
If you notice a duplicate charge, it is automatically detected by our billing
system within 24-48 hours and refunded without needing to contact support.
If it has been longer than 48 hours, please open a support ticket with your
invoice number so our billing team can investigate manually.

Refund Policy
Refunds are issued on a case-by-case basis within 14 days of the original
charge. Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund if canceled within
the first 30 days. Refund requests are reviewed by our billing team and are
not processed automatically by the support agent due to compliance
requirements - these requests should always be escalated to a human agent.

Cancelling Your Subscription
You can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription.
Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. We do not
offer partial-month refunds for mid-cycle cancellations on monthly plans.

Updating Payment Methods
Navigate to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods to add or update a
card. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not
store full card numbers; payment data is handled entirely by our PCI-compliant
payment processor.
